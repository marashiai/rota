The whole change is the diff from the base to the head, with every note in it; each commit shows its own diff and notes the same way. Drafts are saved to your pending review on GitHub as you type; only you see them until you send. With no drafts, the Send button approves: the team may then merge the pull request. A note you have read shows folded, on one line, the next time you open the review, and the read notes on the whole change or a commit fold into a single line: you have read a note once it was on the screen, or once you fold it. New ones say new. On a phone, tap a line or a note, then use the buttons at the bottom.
